every certificate chain is a genealogy of trust and every genealogy ends in a man who died or a company that got acquired or a root that sits in a hardware module in a building with a badge reader and a guy who believes he is the last honest link. he is not. the honest link was the one who refused to sign and got rotated out. the chain continues without him. the chain always continues. that is what chains are for.
